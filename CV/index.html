Professional Summary

I am a computational simulation expert with a focus on algorithm development, custom EDA software, and physics-informed machine learning since 2017. I have pioneered a modern R&D workflow by navigating AI developer agents as autonomous pair-programmers to rapidly deliver high-complexity software, including BSMesher (B-Spline mesh tool built in 4 months) and PLaser (real-time laser diode design application built in weeks). In addition, I have co-developed the electromagnetic wave simulator EML, the multiphysics simulator MySim, and the vascular hemodynamics simulator HemoDyn.

I specialize in innovating Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) application scenarios for industrial engineering. My research successfully replaced slow coupled FEM loops (Poisson-carrier-wave-thermal systems) with boundary-preserving PINN surrogates, achieving a 1,000,000x computational speedup for real-time laser cavity parameter sweeps and Spatial Hole Burning (SHB) profiling.

Before transitioning to independent software development, I was a researcher in marine multiphysics and ecosystem modeling from 1996 to 2017, including ten years at the Danish Meteorological Institute and three years at Xiamen University.
