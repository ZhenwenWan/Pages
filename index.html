About Me

Senior computational scientist and multiphysics modeling expert with over 30 years of experience bridging academic research and industrial software development. My career spans oceanographic CFD, electromagnetic and multiphysics simulations, finite element analysis (FEM), and physics-informed machine learning.

I specialize in navigating AI agents to build complex engineering software. By treating LLM developer agents as autonomous pair-programmers, I have established a state-of-the-art workflow that delivers industrial-grade systems—such as the BSMesher geometry tool and the PLaser EDA platform—in a fraction of traditional development cycles.

Additionally, I focus on innovating Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) application scenarios for industrial design. My research has successfully replaced slow, multi-physics numerical solver iterations with calibrated, boundary-preserving neural network surrogates. This achieves up to a **1,000,000x computational speedup** for real-time laser diode design, spatial hole burning (SHB) optimization, and thermal analysis.

Projects

PLaser (Interactive App) [中文版]

A standalone real-time design application built upon the PINN surrogate framework. By replacing slow iterative numerical solvers with a calibrated neural network, PLaser resolves coupled multi-physics transverse modes, longitudinal carrier depletion (Spatial Hole Burning), self-heating, and optoelectronic thresholds across a 7D design parameter space (including active region width and thickness) inside a 5 ms real-time loop. Features a dark-themed user dashboard with live metrics and 2D mode shape/heat distribution viewports.
